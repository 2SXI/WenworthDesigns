About the workshop

We build one piece at a time, to the measurements of the room it's going into.

Before any wood is cut, we look at the space — how it's used, what it needs to hold, how it should feel — and draw the piece to size. You see the plan before we start, so there aren't any surprises when it arrives.

Every piece is joined and finished by hand in the workshop. We work mostly in local hardwoods, chosen for the piece and the way it will be used, and finish each one to order.

Made to order

Each piece is planned around your brief, not pulled from a catalogue.

Solid wood

Joinery-first construction — mortise, tenon and dovetail where it counts.
